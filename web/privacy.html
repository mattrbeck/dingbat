dingbat

Privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026. This covers dingbat.gg and the dingbat app for iPhone and iPad.

dingbat has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your games and saves stay on your device unless you turn on one of the optional features below.

What stays on your device

The games you add, their saves and save states, your settings, screenshots and clips are kept in the browser's storage for dingbat.gg, or in the app's own storage on iPhone and iPad. A bug report is a file you download and choose where to send; dingbat does not send it anywhere.

Google Drive sync (optional)

Nothing here runs until you sign in with Google. When you do, dingbat asks Google for two things:

  • Its own app-data folder in your Drive. dingbat can read and write only that hidden folder. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Your email address, shown in the app so you can tell which account is signed in. It is kept on your device and used for nothing else.

Your games, saves, save states and synced settings are copied to that folder so your other devices can fetch them. They are stored by Google under your account; dingbat's author has no access to them.

Signing in passes Google's sign-in code and tokens through dingbat's server (signal.dingbat.gg), because Google requires the exchange to be made with a secret that cannot be put in a web page. The server forwards them and does not store them. The tokens are kept on your device (in the Keychain on iPhone and iPad).

To stop: sign out in dingbat, or choose "Sign out everywhere" to revoke dingbat's access for every device. To delete what was synced, open Google Drive's settings, choose Manage apps, and delete dingbat's hidden app data.

dingbat's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Online link cable (optional)

To pair two players, both devices contact dingbat's server with the room code. The server sees each device's IP address and connection details for the moment it takes to pair them. It holds them in memory only while pairing and keeps no record of them.

Setting up the connection also asks a Google-run address service (stun.l.google.com) for your public address, so Google sees your IP address at that moment.

Play then goes directly between the two devices, not through dingbat's server. Your friend's device learns your IP address, as yours learns theirs, and the two exchange what they need to play together: button presses, save data and game data.

Save webhook (optional)

If you enter an address under Settings, dingbat sends each in-game save file there as it is written, with the game's name and the time. dingbat does not run that address; what happens to the file is up to whoever does.

The site and the app

  • dingbat.gg is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address when your browser loads the site; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • dingbat checks dingbat.gg for a newer version and for announcements. These are plain file requests and carry nothing about you.
  • Purchases in the app are handled by Apple. dingbat learns only whether the Supporter purchase exists, never your payment details.
  • The camera, microphone and motion sensors are used only when a game you are playing needs them, and only while it runs. Nothing from them is recorded by dingbat or sent to its author.

Children

dingbat is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
